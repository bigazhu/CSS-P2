Top 5 Bay Area Views

#1: Twin Peaks

Twin Peaks is one of the highest points in San Francisco.

From the top, you can see the whole city and the bay at once.

#2: Mount Tamalpais

Mount Tamalpais has a nice peak across the Golden Gate Bridge.

It offers stunning views of the Bay Area and is popular for hiking, photography, and a view of the pacific ocean.

The rest of my top 5 are Grizzly Peak, Treasure Island, and Indian Rock.
